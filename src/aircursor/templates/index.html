Voice commands (push-to-talk)

Tap Listen and say one exact command: “next slide”, “previous slide”, “volume up”, “volume down”, “play pause”, “mute”, “left click”, “right click”, “double click”, “scroll up” or “scroll down”.

Microphone starts only when you choose Listen.

Browser speech recognition may use a cloud speech service. Voice input requires HTTPS or localhost; do not use for private dictation.
